Questions, answered

What exactly happens in the hour?+
You and Erica meet on a live video call. You walk her through your space, share what you're trying to achieve, and ask anything you're stuck on. She gives you real-time direction: paint, layout, furniture, flow, sourcing, and afterward you receive a written summary with her recommendations.
Do I need to prepare anything?+
Just have your space ready to show on video and a list of what's been frustrating you. The more honest you are about what's not working, the more useful the hour will be. Photos, measurements, and any pieces you're considering are all helpful but not required.
Is one hour really enough?+
For most people, yes, it's enough to get unstuck and walk away with a clear direction. Depending on where you are, it's enough to either start your project with confidence or finish one that's been stalled. And if you want Erica to take it further, your $297 credits toward a full package.
How does the credit work?+
If you decide to upgrade to a Virtual Room Design or any In-Person package, the full $297 you paid for your session applies as a credit toward that service. You're never paying twice for the same work.
Do you only work with Toronto clients?+
Not at all. The Designer Direction Session is virtual, so Erica works with clients anywhere. In-person services are reserved for Midtown Toronto and surrounding neighbourhoods, but the session itself is available wherever you are.
What if I need more help after?+
You'll leave the session knowing your options. If you want ongoing design help, Erica offers Virtual Room Design and full In-Person services, and your session fee credits toward either. There's no pressure; many clients take their direction and run with it themselves.
